# Historical extended-ratio ablation diagnostic

These tables contain the historical seven-configuration diagnostic. Their macro accuracy averages 14 drift ratios, including 30% and 50%. The paper ablation uses V, VT, and V+VT (code groups A, E_text, and A+E_text), averaged over 12 ratios from 0% through 20%. Its canonical table is `../../论文消融结果表.csv` and the matching English table is `../figures/paper_ablation_table.md`.
